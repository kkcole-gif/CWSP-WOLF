What this certificate will address

Teaching oral communication asks more of you than assigning a presentation. You need to know how speaking conventions differ across disciplines and how communication apprehension affects performance and learning. You also need ways to assess oral work that help students grow as communicators.

The Oral Communication Instruction certificate will give faculty frameworks for designing scaffolded speaking assignments, rubrics that distinguish skill development from nervousness, and strategies for teaching disciplinary oral communication, from STEM presentations to clinical interviews to leading a seminar discussion.

Planned structure

Core modules (in development)

  • 1

    Principles of Public Speaking Instruction

    Classical rhetoric, effective speech structure, communication apprehension reduction, and scaffolded speaking assignments. How to teach improvisation and build speaker confidence across a semester.

    Weeks 1–2 · Workbook in development

  • 2

    Oral Communication Across Disciplines

    How speaking conventions differ across STEM, humanities, and social sciences, and across formal and informal contexts. Code-switching, field-specific presentation models, and designing speaking assignments that engage authentic disciplinary practice.

    Weeks 3–4 · Workbook in development

  • 3

    Assessing Oral Communication

    Rubric design for oral performance, peer evaluation models, and strategies for cutting grading time while keeping feedback strong. How to distinguish communication skill from performance anxiety in assessment.

    Weeks 5–6 · Workbook in development

Planned electives

Choose two of four (when available)

Interpersonal and Group Communication Strategies

Facilitating discussion, building group dynamics, conflict resolution, and designing activities that develop interpersonal communication skills.

Visual and Media Aids in Oral Presentations

Slide design principles, effective media integration, avoiding cognitive overload, and how to teach students to speak from visual support without reading it aloud.

Creating Engaging Speaking Assignments

Designing assignments that build creativity, engagement, and authentic audience awareness. Peer review of student speaking assignment designs.

Multilingual Considerations in Oral Communication

Speech challenges for L2 learners, cultural speaking norms, accessibility, and strategies for inclusive speaking instruction.
