Apply

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. There is no cost to apply or participate.

Who is eligible

C²TC is open to all NC State instructional faculty, including tenure-track, professional-track, and part-time faculty. Graduate students with teaching responsibilities are also eligible to apply.

No certificate has a prerequisite during the pilot. CWSP has waived the Core Teaching Certificate (CTC) requirement for pilot participants.

What applying does

There is no selection process. The application registers you with CWSP, so that we know which certificate you are working on, can send you cohort session dates, and can set up the Curriculum Development Stipend if you take it on. The form asks for your Faculty ID and your department head, because CWSP confirms the stipend with your department head before the work begins. Every workbook is open now, so you can start today, before or after you apply.

Faculty in any C²TC cohort are eligible to apply for a $500 C²TC Curriculum Development Stipend. The stipend pays for curriculum work the program needs, separate from your regular teaching assignment, in two parts: one teaching resource for the C²TC repository, written so that colleagues in other departments can adapt it, and a structured review of two modules, completed in a working session with the CWSP co-directors. Completing a certificate or redesigning your own course does not qualify on its own, since that work falls within your teaching. CWSP confirms the scope of the work with your department head before you begin, and pays the stipend through your department once you submit both pieces.

Choosing your certificate

Apply for one certificate, several, or all three at once. If you are unsure where to start, choose Written Communication, which prepares you well for the others. The Digital Communication certificate suits faculty who already assign digital work. The Oral Communication certificate is in development. Early applicants go on a waitlist, and CWSP will notify them when the modules open.

Ready to apply

Application form

The application takes about 5 minutes. The form asks for your name, department, faculty status, Faculty ID and department head (for the Curriculum Development Stipend), the certificate or certificates you plan to start, and a short statement (2 to 3 sentences) on what you hope to gain from the program.

Open application form (opens in a new window)

The form requires an NC State Google sign-in. Check “Send me a copy of my responses” at the end to keep a record.

Quick facts

Cost

Free for all NC State instructional faculty and eligible graduate students

Format

Asynchronous self-paced workbooks with optional synchronous cohort touchpoints (4 per semester)

Time

19 to 28 hours per certificate (three core modules, one elective, and the capstone), over six to eight weeks or one semester

Certificate

PDF certificate emailed once CWSP has reviewed and approved your capstone. Complete all three for the C²TC Certificate of Advanced Communication Pedagogy.

Curriculum stipend

$500 for faculty who develop a teaching resource for the repository and review two modules
