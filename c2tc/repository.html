Teaching Materials Repository

Teaching resources from CWSP, along with a growing collection of materials from C²TC participants: assignment prompts, rubrics, capstone projects, and other course materials from instructors across disciplines.

CWSP Sample Materials

Developed by the Campus Writing and Speaking Program

CWSP wrote these materials for NC State faculty. The C²TC workbooks refer to them, and you are welcome to use them in your teaching and course design.

Sample Assignment with Evaluation Guide

A one-page critique assignment from a history of science course, with a three-level evaluation guide (strong, average, weak). Use it with the Rubric Workshop in WC Module 2.

An overview of feedback and grading methods, including ungrading, labor-based grading, and contract grading, with sources from the research.

Response Resources

CWSP Response Style Overview

An overview of the response styles CWSP describes, with examples of each and the situations each suits.

Responding to Student Projects Orally

Sample Infographics (Digital Communication)
