What this certificate addresses

Faculty across disciplines face a common challenge: students arrive with writing experience but struggle to apply it in new contexts. The Written Communication Instruction Certificate gives faculty the theoretical tools and practical strategies to understand why that happens — and to design courses that close the gap.

The three core modules build sequentially: Module 1 establishes the research foundations of writing pedagogy; Module 2 develops feedback and assessment practices that produce revision rather than just evaluation; Module 3 connects writing instruction to the specific knowledge-making practices of your discipline. Electives allow you to go deeper in areas most relevant to your teaching.

Core modules

The required sequence

  • 1

    Foundations of Writing Pedagogy

    Why writing doesn't transfer automatically between contexts, how metacognition shapes writing development, and what the research says about process-based versus product-based approaches. Introduces the frameworks that anchor the rest of the certificate.

    Weeks 1–2 · ~4–6 hours

    Open Module 1 workbook →
  • 2

    Feedback and Assessment in Writing

    How feedback practices shape what students do — and don't — learn. Covers minimal marking, screencast and audio feedback, rubric design, and peer review as a pedagogy rather than an efficiency tool.

    Weeks 3–4 · ~5–7 hours

    Open Module 2 workbook →
  • 3

    Discipline-Specific Writing Instruction

    How writing functions within your specific discipline — the genres, conventions, and discourse features that define knowledge-making in your field. Uses the DAPOE framework for assignment design and genre theory to help faculty build authentic writing tasks.

    Weeks 5–6 · ~5–7 hours

    Open Module 3 workbook →

Capstone

Develop a writing-intensive syllabus or curriculum plan

Your capstone applies the certificate's frameworks to your actual course or program. You submit a syllabus or curriculum plan incorporating research-based communication design, along with a reflective rationale explaining your choices. Strong capstones are eligible for presentation at the OFE Annual Conference.

Capstone submission is part of the Module 3 workbook. Upon submission, your certificate is generated and emailed to you automatically.
