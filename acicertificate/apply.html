Apply

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. There is no cost to apply or participate.

Who is eligible

C²TC is open to all NC State instructional faculty, including tenure-track, professional-track, and part-time faculty. Graduate students with teaching responsibilities are also eligible to apply.

The Written Communication, Digital Communication, and Oral Communication certificates have no prerequisite. Some program materials list the Core Teaching Certificate (CTC) as a prerequisite. CWSP has waived that requirement for pilot participants.

What to expect after applying

After you submit the application form, you will receive a confirmation email. Within one week, CWSP will tell you whether you are accepted and give you access to the workbooks. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis, with no competitive review.

Beta participants in the first cohort are eligible for a $500 mini-grant to work with the CWSP co-directors on curriculum development and program refinement. The mini-grant adds one meeting per semester. You also have the option to help write elective or capstone materials.

Choosing your certificate

Apply for one certificate, several, or all three at once. If you are unsure where to start, choose Written Communication. It is the most developed of the three and prepares you well for the others. The Digital Communication certificate suits faculty who already assign digital work. The Oral Communication certificate is in development. Early applicants go on a waitlist, and CWSP will notify them when the modules open.

Ready to apply

Application form

The application takes about 5 minutes. The form asks for your name, department, faculty status, the certificate or certificates you are applying for, and a short statement (2–3 sentences) on what you hope to gain from the program.

Quick facts

Cost

Free for all NC State instructional faculty and eligible graduate students

Format

Asynchronous self-paced workbooks with optional synchronous cohort touchpoints (4 per semester)

Time

About 20 hours per certificate (three core modules and two electives); four to six weeks with steady effort, or one semester

Certificate

PDF certificate emailed once CWSP has reviewed and approved your capstone. Complete all three for the C²TC Certificate of Advanced Communication Pedagogy.

Beta grant

$500 mini-grant for beta participants in the first cohort who help develop the program
