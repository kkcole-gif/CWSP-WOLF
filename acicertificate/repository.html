CWSP Sample Materials

Developed by the Campus Writing and Speaking Program

These materials were created by CWSP for NC State faculty. They are referenced directly in the C²TC workbooks and are available here for use in your teaching and course design.

Assessment Tools

CWSP Sample Analytical Rubric

A genre-specific rubric model for written communication assignments; used in WC Module 2 for the rubric critique activity.

Download PDF

CWSP Formative Criteria Sheet

A formative assessment tool that describes writing in process rather than evaluating a final product; alternative to traditional rubric formats.

Download PDF

CWSP Response Tools

A practical overview of feedback methods — including ungrading, labor-based grading, and contract grading — grounded in current research.

Download PDF

Response Resources

CWSP Response Style Overview

The foundational document for the four CWSP response styles — expressive, impressionistic, criterion-based, and descriptive — with examples and use cases for each.

Download PDF

Error Tips — Suggestions for Handling Errors in Student Writing

Research-grounded guidance on prioritizing error feedback, distinguishing types of error, and avoiding over-correction. Emphasizes pattern-level feedback over instance-level markup.

Download PDF

Top 10 Errors Faculty Notice

A cross-disciplinary inventory of the errors most consistently flagged by NC State faculty — with pedagogical implications for each pattern.

Download PDF

Peer Review Tools

Modes of Peer Review

A visual overview of peer review formats — including author-centered, reviewer-centered, and structured protocols — with strengths and limitations of each mode.

Download PDF

Peer Tips

Faculty-facing guidance for implementing effective peer review — how to train students as reviewers, structure review sessions, and make feedback actionable.

Download PDF

Formal Assignment Peer Review Activity

A sample structured peer review protocol that be adapted for use in any course. Includes reviewer framing, task sequence, and writer response prompts.

Download PDF

Peerwriting

Strategies for integrating peer writing response into asynchronous and hybrid course formats, including low-stakes and high-stakes peer review variations.
