NC State Campus Writing and Speaking Program

RED-ACI

Reflective Educational Design — Advanced Communication Instruction

A free, asynchronous certificate program for NC State faculty who want to deepen their expertise in writing, oral, and digital communication pedagogy.

Apply now Explore certificates

Cost

Free

No cost to NC State faculty or eligible graduate students

Format

Asynchronous

Self-paced with optional synchronous cohort touchpoints

Time

10–12 hours

Per certificate; completable in 4 weeks or one semester for all three

Beta grant

$500 mini-grant

Early enrollees collaborate with CWSP co-directors on curriculum development

Three specialized certificates

Choose your focus — or earn all three

Each certificate stands on its own. Complete all three to earn the RED Certificate of Advanced Communication Pedagogy. The prerequisite for any certificate is the RED Core Teaching Certificate.

Certificate 1

Written Communication Instruction

Research-based frameworks for teaching writing across disciplines. Covers writing transfer, feedback and assessment, discipline-specific genre instruction, and assignment design.

3 core modules + 2 electives Capstone: writing-intensive syllabus or curriculum plan
Explore certificate →
Certificate 2

Digital Communication Instruction

Frameworks for teaching digital rhetoric, multimodal composition, and digital project design. Covers digital literacies, assessment, and ethical dimensions of digital communication.

3 core modules + 2 electives Capstone: digital portfolio assignment or module

What you gain

Built for faculty who teach communication

RED-ACI is designed for faculty who already take their teaching seriously and want more sophisticated tools. It is not a remediation program. It is an advanced professional development experience grounded in communication studies research.

Knowledge and skills

  • → Research-based strategies for teaching written, oral, and digital communication
  • → Assessment tools and rubrics for communication-intensive assignments
  • → AI-assisted instruction and multimodal composition frameworks
  • → Inclusive and accessible design for diverse learners

Recognition and community

  • → Specialized certificates and digital badges for professional portfolios
  • → Network of faculty across disciplines interested in communication pedagogy
  • → Opportunity to present capstone projects at the OFE Annual Conference
  • → Access to a shared repository of faculty-created teaching materials

Related program

Looking for course-level support?

WOLF (Written and Oral Literacy Facilitation) pairs faculty with a CWSP liaison to audit and redesign communication in a specific course. RED-ACI and WOLF are designed to complement each other.
