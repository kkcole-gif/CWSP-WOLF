Cohort Schedule

RED-ACI is self-paced, but you are not working alone. Each semester cohort includes four synchronous touchpoints — optional but strongly encouraged — at predictable intervals.

How the cohort model works

The certificate modules are fully asynchronous — you complete them on your own schedule, at whatever time of day works for you. But professional development is stronger when it happens in community. RED-ACI cohorts are organized each semester so that participants who start at the same time can meet four times across the semester for structured peer exchange.

Touchpoints are not content delivery sessions. They are working sessions: you bring what you have made, discuss what surprised you, get feedback on a design you are developing, and hear how colleagues in other disciplines are applying the same frameworks. CWSP facilitates and occasionally brings a short provocation, but the work is yours.

Touchpoints are approximately 60–75 minutes and are held via Zoom. Recordings are available to participants who cannot attend.

Semester timeline

One certificate in one semester

W1

Week 1 — Orientation

Welcome email with cohort roster and Zoom link. Asynchronous orientation materials: how to navigate the workbooks, what to expect from touchpoints, and how the certificate submission works. Module 1 workbook opens.

W2

Week 2 — Touchpoint 1: Foundations

First synchronous session. Focus: your current assumptions about communication instruction and what Module 1 challenged. Short pair activity — share one insight and one question from the Assumptions Audit. CWSP offers a brief provocation. (~60 min)

W3

Weeks 3–4 — Module 2

Module 2 workbook: Feedback and Assessment. Complete the Feedback Comparison Analysis and begin the Screencast Feedback practice activity. Module 1 reflection due by end of Week 3 if submitting for cohort feedback.

W4

Week 4 — Touchpoint 2: Feedback Practices

Bring a real piece of student writing (anonymized) and the feedback you gave on it. Small group discussion: what did your feedback prioritize? What would you do differently using the Module 2 frameworks? (~75 min)

W5–7

Weeks 5–7 — Module 3 and Electives

Module 3 workbook: Discipline-Specific Writing Instruction. Begin elective workbooks (choose 2). Genre Analysis activity in your own discipline. Begin drafting your capstone syllabus or curriculum plan.

W8

Week 8 — Touchpoint 3: Assignment Design Workshop

Bring a draft assignment you are designing (or revising) using the Module 3 frameworks. Structured peer feedback using the DAPOE criteria. CWSP facilitates a gallery walk format. (~75 min)

W9–11

Weeks 9–11 — Electives and Capstone Development

Complete your two elective workbooks. Develop your capstone project: writing-intensive syllabus or curriculum plan with reflective rationale. Optional: submit a draft to CWSP for written feedback before final submission.

W12

Week 12 — Touchpoint 4: Capstone Showcase

Final synchronous session. Participants share a 3–5 minute overview of their capstone project. Audience asks questions and offers observations. CWSP discusses OFE Annual Conference submission for strong capstones. Celebration. (~75 min)

W13–14

Weeks 13–14 — Capstone Submission and Certificate

Final capstone submission through the Module 3 workbook. Certificate generated and emailed automatically upon submission. Participation in the optional repository contribution step — share your capstone materials with the faculty repository.

All three certificates in one year

One certificate per semester

Participants who want to earn the full RED Certificate of Advanced Communication Pedagogy can complete one certificate per semester across three semesters, or complete all three simultaneously in a single semester (approximately 30–36 hours of total work). Most participants who complete all three in one semester do so by overlapping the elective workbooks rather than doing all modules strictly sequentially.
