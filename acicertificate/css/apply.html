Apply

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. There is no cost to apply or participate.

Who is eligible

RED-ACI is open to all NC State instructional faculty, including tenure-track, professional-track, and part-time faculty. Graduate students with teaching responsibilities are also eligible to apply.

There is no prerequisite for the Written Communication, Digital Communication, or Oral Communication certificates. (The RED Core Teaching Certificate is listed as a prerequisite in some program materials — this requirement is currently waived for pilot participants.)

What to expect after applying

Once you submit the application form, you will receive a confirmation email. Participants are notified of acceptance and provided workbook access within one week of submitting. Most participants are accepted on a rolling basis; there is no competitive review.

Beta participants — those who apply before Spring 2026 cohort closes — are eligible for a $500 mini-grant to collaborate directly with CWSP co-directors on curriculum development and program refinement. Mini-grant participation involves one additional meeting per semester and optional co-development of elective or capstone materials.

Choosing your certificate

You can apply for one certificate, multiple certificates, or all three at once. If you are unsure which to start with, the Written Communication certificate is the most developed and is a strong foundation for the others. The Digital Communication certificate is a natural companion for faculty already integrating digital assignments. The Oral Communication certificate is in development — early applicants will be added to the waitlist and notified when modules are available.

Ready to apply

Application form

The application takes approximately 5 minutes. You will be asked for your name, department, faculty status, which certificate(s) you are applying for, and a brief statement (2–3 sentences) on what you hope to gain from the program.

Quick facts

Cost

Free for all NC State instructional faculty and eligible graduate students

Format

Asynchronous self-paced workbooks with optional synchronous cohort touchpoints (4 per semester)

Time

10–12 hours per certificate; 4 weeks with focused effort or one full semester

Certificate

PDF certificate emailed automatically upon capstone submission. Complete all three for the RED Certificate of Advanced Communication Pedagogy.

Beta grant

$500 mini-grant available for early enrollees who collaborate on program development
