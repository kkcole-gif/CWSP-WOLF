Faculty Repository

Assignments, rubrics, and teaching materials contributed by faculty who have completed RED-ACI modules. Browse and adapt for your own courses.

About the repository

The RED-ACI repository is a shared, searchable collection of faculty-created teaching materials. Every workbook in the program includes an optional submission step: if you are willing to share what you have designed, it becomes part of this growing collection.

Materials are submitted with a brief description and the faculty member's name and department (or anonymous, if preferred). They are reviewed by CWSP before being added to the repository. Attribution is included unless the contributor requests otherwise.

The repository is organized by certificate track and material type. All materials are free to use and adapt for your own courses. If you adapt a shared material significantly, you are encouraged to contribute your revised version back.

Repository status

The RED-ACI program is currently in beta. The repository will be populated as early enrollees complete modules and contribute materials. If you are an early enrollee, your capstone submission is automatically added here unless you opt out during submission.
