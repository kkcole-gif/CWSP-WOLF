For Liaisons

For the faculty member who runs WOLF in a department or program. One workbook carries the work from first consultation to certification, and the department's Writing and Speaking Curriculum Plan comes out of it.

Open the WOLF Workbook Schedule the first consultation

What the liaison does

The liaison is the point of contact between a department and CWSP. You facilitate; you do not produce everything yourself. You connect CWSP to your colleagues, translate disciplinary context into curriculum decisions, and keep the work moving between consultations. You need institutional knowledge of your department and enough trust from colleagues to hold an honest conversation about the curriculum. Expertise in writing pedagogy is CWSP's job. The plan that results belongs to your department, and departments that complete the program earn CWSP certification and a $1,000 mini grant.

WOLF is patterned on the University of Minnesota's Writing-Enriched Curriculum model: faculty-driven, department-owned, built over two to three years at the department's own pace.

Stage 1 · Inquiry
Where are we?

Confirm your role, gather syllabi, run a short needs assessment, find where AI already sits in the curriculum, and reflect on what the inquiry showed.

Produces a picture of the curriculum as it stands, filed with CWSP.
Stage 2 · Design
What should be different?

With CWSP, faculty write the discipline's writing and speaking outcomes by level, map them to courses, and fold in AI literacy outcomes.

Produces the Writing and Speaking Curriculum Plan, generated from your answers, ready for the chair to approve.
Stage 3 · Evidence
How do we know it is working?

Implement the plan, collect assessment evidence with adapted rubrics, nominate colleagues for recognition, and submit the certification documents.

Produces certification, recognition letters, and the mini grant.

How the workbook works

  • Work in the browser. It saves as you type, and "Save and email me a link" lets you continue on any computer.
  • Each stage ends with a submission. CWSP receives it, files a formatted copy in your department's folder, and emails the copy to you as a Word file and a PDF.
  • The sidebar shows which stage is current, which are done, and which lie ahead, from the dates you set.
  • All three stages are open. A department works through them over two to three years, and each submission is kept.
