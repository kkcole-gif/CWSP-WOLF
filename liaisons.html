For Liaisons

All working documents, guides, templates, and supplementary resources for CWSP liaisons facilitating WOLF engagements.

Core documents

Working resources for every phase of WOLF

These documents support liaisons across the Inquiry, Design, and Evidence phases. Each links to the live Google Doc — open it, make a copy, and adapt for your engagement.

  • 📋
    01 — WOLF Program Overview

    The full program description: what WOLF is, what it is not, the three-phase model, and how to orient faculty partners at the start of an engagement.

    Open document →
  • 🗂️
    02 — Liaison Guide

    A structured guide to facilitating WOLF from first contact through final documentation. Covers meeting protocols, question banks, and how to move between phases.

    Open document →
  • 📝
    03 — WSCP Working Template

    The shared working document used throughout a WOLF engagement. Tracks inquiry findings, design decisions, assignment drafts, and evidence collected. One copy per faculty engagement.

    Open document →
  • 🏆
    04 — Assessment and Recognition

    Rubrics, self-assessment tools, and program-level evidence frameworks. Includes sample rubrics for written and oral communication and a liaison self-assessment instrument.

    Open document →

Additional tools and strategies

Standalone resources for specific liaison tasks. Use these as needed across your engagement.

  • 🔧
    Scaffolding Strategies

    A practical toolkit for designing scaffolded communication tasks: sequencing approaches, low-stakes assignment types, feedback structures, and models across disciplines.

    Open document →
  • 📊

    Discipline-adaptable rubrics for written assignments, oral presentations, and multimodal projects. Each is annotated with guidance on how to calibrate criteria for specific course contexts.
