Teaching writing and speaking in every discipline at NC State

The Campus Writing and Speaking Program works with faculty, departments, and colleges on the writing and speaking their students do. Start from the work in front of you.

CWSP is part of the Office for Faculty Excellence. See CWSP on the OFE site

What are you working on?

  • One course I teach

    C²TC, the Communication Teaching Certificate

    Free, self-paced certificates in written, digital, and oral communication instruction, each 19 to 28 hours, ending in a capstone you design for a course you teach.

    Explore C²TC

  • My department's curriculum

    WOLF, Written and Oral Literacy Facilitation

    A two-to-three-year partnership that builds writing and speaking into a department, program, or college curriculum, led by up to three faculty liaisons the unit chooses.

    Explore WOLF
  • Conceptual Structural Operative

    AI in a course, program, or department

    AI Literacies and the AI Policy Lab

    A three-level framework, a faculty workbook, a policy workshop, and the AI Practice Adventure, for deciding where AI belongs in your teaching and putting the decision in writing.

    Explore the AI programs
  • CWSP sessions Wednesdays 1:15 to 2:15 pm, Primrose 101 and Zoom

    A session or time to write

    Events and writing challenges

    Wednesday sessions, Faculty Conversations, and writing challenges, open to everyone who teaches, with no certificate or partnership required.
