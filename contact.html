Talk with CWSP

The Campus Writing and Speaking Program works with NC State faculty, graduate instructors, departments, and programs on the teaching of writing and speaking in every discipline. Write to the co-director who leads the work you have in mind, or to either of us if your question crosses programs.

Where to begin

A department or program curriculum

WOLF partners with departments, degree programs, and groups of faculty to build a writing and speaking curriculum plan, led by up to three liaisons the unit chooses and usually completed over two to three years. Start with the intake form, which takes about three minutes and requires NC State sign-in.

A Communication Teaching Certificate

C²TC is free for NC State instructional faculty and graduate instructors. Questions about the Written and Digital Communication certificates, the cohort sessions, or the Curriculum Development Stipend go to Kirsti Cole. Questions about the Oral Communication certificate, now in development, go to Roy Schwartzman.

Apply to C²TC C²TC overview Ask Roy about the Oral certificate

AI literacy and AI policy

CWSP offers the AI Literacies extension for departments, the AI Policy Lab for committees writing or revising AI use policy at the course, program, or institutional level, and the AI Practice Adventure for individual instructors working out their own approach. Write to Kirsti Cole to plan any of these, on its own or alongside a WOLF engagement.

AI Literacies AI Policy Lab AI Practice Adventure

A single course or assignment

Instructors revising one assignment, a rubric, a peer review sequence, or the speaking component of a course are welcome to request a consultation. Write to either co-director with the course and the assignment you are working on.

Workshops and events

CWSP sessions are open to all NC State instructional faculty and graduate students who teach, whether or not they are working toward a certificate.

See upcoming events Subscribe to the CWSP calendar

After you write

A co-director replies within two business days and proposes a first conversation about your courses, your students, and the writing and speaking you ask of them. The first conversation commits you to nothing, and no background in writing or speaking pedagogy is needed to request one.
