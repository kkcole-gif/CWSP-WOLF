Teaching Materials Repository

A growing collection of faculty-created materials from C²TC participants — assignment prompts, rubrics, capstone projects, and course artifacts contributed by instructors across disciplines.

How the repository works

The repository collects materials produced through the C²TC program and submitted voluntarily by participants. Everything here was created by NC State faculty or graduate students as part of their certificate work — meaning it reflects real disciplinary contexts, actual course design decisions, and the pedagogical frameworks the certificate teaches. Materials are shared with attribution and under the program's Creative Commons license.

The collection grows as cohorts complete their capstone projects. If you have completed a C²TC certificate and want to contribute a rubric, assignment sequence, or capstone artifact, use the submission link below. CWSP reviews all submissions before they appear in the repository.
