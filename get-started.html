Get Started with WOLF

A WOLF engagement starts with a conversation, and you decide afterward whether to continue. Here is what to expect.

The process

What happens when you reach out

Initial conversation

Tell CWSP about your department, program, or group through the intake form, or by email. Kirsti Cole follows up to set a first conversation about your curriculum, your students, and the writing and speaking you expect of them. The first meeting is exploratory and commits you to nothing. You need no background in writing pedagogy.

Scoping the engagement

Together we decide what the engagement covers: a department's full curriculum, one degree program, a college-wide effort, or a set of related courses taught by a group of faculty. We set a rough timeline and a few goals.

Choosing liaisons

The department chooses up to three of its own faculty as liaisons. They lead the work locally, with Kirsti Cole and Roy Schwartzman of CWSP.

Working through the stages

Liaisons work through the Inquiry, Design, and Evidence stages in the WOLF Workbook, meeting with CWSP at each stage. A department moves at its own pace, usually over two to three years.

Plan and certification

The workbook produces the Writing and Speaking Curriculum Plan, which belongs to the department and goes to the chair for approval. Departments that complete Stage 3 earn CWSP certification, and each liaison (up to three per department or program) receives $1,000.

A few things to know

Before you reach out

You do not need to have a problem

Many departments bring curricula that already work well. They come to strengthen what they do or to document it.

Any discipline is welcome

WOLF has worked with courses in engineering, business, social sciences, humanities, natural sciences, and professional programs. Each discipline communicates in its own way, so CWSP brings frameworks your department adapts to its field.

Timing matters

The most productive engagements start a semester or more before a program review or curriculum revision. Write to us early.

AI Literacies is available

If your interest is specifically in AI, authorship, and academic integrity, tell us. We will plan an engagement around the AI Literacies extension or combine it with a full WOLF engagement.

AI Policy Lab is available

If your department or curriculum committee needs to write or revise AI use policy (at the course, program, or institutional level), the AI Policy Lab is a standalone workshop. It runs on its own or alongside a WOLF engagement.

Also from CWSP

C²TC Communication Teaching Certificate

Faculty who want deeper expertise in communication pedagogy earn a certificate through CWSP's self-paced professional development program. Three certificate tracks cover written, digital, and oral communication instruction, each built around research-based frameworks and your own course materials.
