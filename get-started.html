Get Started with WOLF

A WOLF engagement starts with a conversation, and you decide afterward whether to continue. Here is what to expect.

The process

What happens when you reach out

Initial conversation

We talk about your course, your students, and the communication expectations you hold for them. The first meeting is exploratory. You need no background in writing pedagogy.

Scoping the engagement

Together we decide what a WOLF engagement should cover for you: a single assignment redesign, a curriculum audit, or something in between. We set a rough timeline and a few goals.

Liaison matching

CWSP staff match you with a liaison suited to your discipline and your schedule.

Working sessions

You and your liaison work through the Inquiry, Design, and Evidence phases at a pace that fits your semester. Most engagements involve four to six working sessions of one to two hours each.

Documentation and close

Your WSCP Working Template records the work of the engagement: the decisions you made, the materials you produced, and the evidence you gathered. You keep it.

A few things to know

Before you reach out

You do not need to have a problem

Many faculty bring courses that already work well. They come to strengthen what they do or to document it.

Any discipline is welcome

WOLF has worked with courses in engineering, business, social sciences, humanities, natural sciences, and professional programs. Each discipline communicates in its own way, so liaisons bring frameworks you adapt to your field.

Timing matters

The most productive engagements begin a semester before the course you want to revise. A mid-semester redesign works on a compressed schedule. Write to us early.

AI Literacies is available

If your interest is specifically in AI, authorship, and academic integrity, tell us. We will plan an engagement around the AI Literacies extension or combine it with a full WOLF engagement.

AI Policy Lab is available

If your department or curriculum committee needs to write or revise AI use policy (at the course, program, or institutional level), the AI Policy Lab is a standalone workshop. It runs on its own or alongside a WOLF engagement.

Also from CWSP

C²TC Communication Teaching Certificate

Faculty who want deeper expertise in communication pedagogy earn a certificate through CWSP's self-paced professional development program. Three certificate tracks cover written, digital, and oral communication instruction, each built around research-based frameworks and your own course materials.
